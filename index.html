      Volunteer sign-in

      Use the US mobile number on your volunteer record. Only approved volunteers can submit updates.

      Phone number not found

      Ask an administrator to add your US mobile number to your active volunteer profile first. Having your name on the schedule does not mean your sign-in number has been added.

      No code, or unable to sign in?

      If no text arrives, check the number and ask your administrator to confirm it is saved on your profile. Repeatedly requesting codes will not fix a missing profile number.

      If a code expires, request a new one and enter only the latest text code. If it still fails, contact your coordinator with the exact error message. Never share your code.

      My availability or assignment is incorrect

      You do not need to sign in to ask for a correction. Contact your coordinator with the date you are available from and the shifts that need changing. A coordinator or administrator can update assignments; an administrator can update profile availability notes.

      Signing in does not give volunteers permission to edit their own profile or recurring assignments. Use Need backup on your assigned upcoming shift if temporary cover is needed.

      By choosing Send text code, you agree to receive a sign-in text. Message and data rates may apply. Google processes your phone number for verification and abuse prevention. See our privacy information below.
